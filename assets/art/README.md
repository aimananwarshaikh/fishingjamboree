# Art

Source art, 2250x1650 for cards, 1500x1500 for tokens. Renamed from the original Drive zips.
Cards and backs are for Milestone B (real UI); Milestone A doesn't use them.

- `cards/safe-risk/` — Safe and Risk deck faces (Boot, +1/+2/+3 fish, fines, big fish, Shark, Sinking Boat)
- `cards/event/` — Event deck faces (Golden Fish per fishing spot and +1 Catch per zone, movement, Helicopter, Shark Repellent, Tsunami)
- `cards/backs/` — safe (blue), risk (red), event (yellow)
- `tokens/` — fish ($100–$1000), fine, Speed/Capacity upgrades, round counter
- `money/` — $100 and $500 bills

Notes: the original `Token Counter (2).jpg` is actually the $100 fish token, saved as `tokens/fish-100.jpg`.
The original `14-Event-Card--Lose-Fish` is the Sinking Boat card (a Risk card, not an event).
Old file names used zone letters A/B/C; renamed to 1/2/3 (A=1, B=2, C=3, confirmed). Golden Fish files are `spot-N`, +1 Catch files are `zone-N`.
Shipwreck Bay and Davy Jones' Locker have no art of their own; they exist on the board only.
The board image is separate, in `assets/board/`.
