# Independent review (Session 7, stage 7a)

Written 2026-11-13 for the 17 Nov handoff. Nothing was fixed; this is a list for stage 7b and Milestone B.

## Summary

- **No rule bug found.** I read every rule in `docs/rulebook.md` and every row of the notes log against the engine and found no place where the engine plays differently from the rulebook.
- **The risks are around the game, not in it.** The two P1 items are about a player losing their seat and a game that can be held up. Neither needs an engine change.
- **Priorities:** P1 = could spoil the first playtest. P2 = fix before playtests with people outside the team, or a design decision the designer should confirm. P3 = cleanup, hardening and missing tests.

How each finding was checked is stated in it: **read** (from the code), or **run** (I measured it with a throwaway script that is not in the repo). The scripts played random games through the real engine: 300 four-player games with one seat played by `chooseAutoAction`, and 1,000 games of 2–4 random players.

## P1

### 1. A player who closes their tab cannot come back

- **Where:** `apps/web/src/useGame.ts:31-47` (seat token kept in `sessionStorage`), `packages/server/src/core.ts:243-245` (no joining once the game has started), `core.ts:263-264` (the token is the only way back).
- **What happens:** the seat token lives only in that tab. Closing the tab, opening the link in another browser or on another device, or a phone browser that throws the tab away, loses it. There is no rejoin by name and no way to show or copy the token. The server then plays that seat for the rest of the game (see P2-3 for how badly).
- **Checked by:** read. A refresh in the same tab does work (tested in 6b).
- **Note:** `docs/protocol.md` §2 suggests `localStorage`. The client chose `sessionStorage` so two tabs can be two players, which matters for testing on one machine but not for real players.

### 2. One player can hold the game up for as long as they like

- **Where:** `packages/server/src/core.ts:415-431` (the only timers are for disconnected seats), `core.ts:530-537` (`vacate` starts a fresh 60 seconds on every disconnect).
- **What happens:**
  - A connected player who does not act is never played for. There is no turn timer, no host kick and no pause (listed as open in `docs/protocol.md` §10).
  - A player with a flaky connection gets a new 60-second grace on every drop, with no total budget, so the server may never take over.
  - A phone that locks mid-turn costs up to about 90 seconds per decision: up to 30 seconds for the heartbeat to notice, then 60 seconds of grace.
- **Checked by:** read.
- **Why P1:** on a voice call the group can talk it through, but the only real fix available to them is to abandon the room, and with item 1 that loses the game.

## P2

### 3. The server's play for an away player costs them the game

- **Where:** `packages/server/src/autoplay.ts:24-30`.
- **What happens:** "the first legal destination that is not a risk tile" means the lowest tile id, so the ship sails to the top-left corner and shuttles between (2,0) and (3,0). It draws a Safe card every turn, collects fines, and almost never docks.
- **Checked by:** run. Over 300 four-player games the away seat ended its turn on (2,0) or (3,0) in 2,705 of 6,000 turns, on a dock in 45, and finished on **−$220** on average.
- **Why it matters:** the protocol says the server does "as little as possible" for the player, but drawing cards is not little. A player who drops for ten minutes comes back to a ship in the far corner holding fine tokens. Options for the designer: head for the nearest dock and stay near it, or skip the draw. Either is a rule decision.

### 4. One failing room stops the timers for other rooms

- **Where:** `packages/server/src/core.ts:172-181` (`tick`), `core.ts:442-451` (`autoAct` throws on purpose), `packages/server/src/transport.ts:138-151`.
- **What happens:** `tick` walks every room in one loop with one shared `out` list. If any room throws:
  - rooms later in the loop are not processed, on this tick or any later one, because the transport retries every second and the same room throws again;
  - updates already built for earlier rooms in that tick are dropped, although their actions were applied. Those clients miss an update and only recover through the `seq` gap on the next one.
- **Checked by:** read. I found no input that makes the engine throw today (the fuzz test supports that), so this needs an engine or core bug to trigger. The cost if it does is every room on the server.
- **Related:** an exception in `handleMessage` (`core.ts:353`) is logged and the sender gets no reply. The client then keeps its buttons disabled (`apps/web/src/App.tsx:206`, `state.pending`) until the page is refreshed.

### 5. A server restart ends every game

- **Where:** `packages/server/src/core.ts:112` (rooms are a `Map`), `core.ts:207-210` (the replay log exists but is never saved).
- **What happens:** a crash, a redeploy or a host that sleeps the process loses all rooms. The client then gets `room-not-found` and is sent back to the start screen (`apps/web/src/useGame.ts:117-122`).
- **Checked by:** read. Known and listed in `docs/protocol.md` §10; raised here because the replay log already holds everything needed to restore a game.

### 6. Design points for the designer to confirm

These follow the rulebook as written. I flag them because playtesters will hit them.

- **The left starting dock has one exit.** (3,19) opens only onto (3,18) (`content/board.json`). A ship that ends its move on (3,18) shuts the left dock in both directions for a turn: the docked ship misses its turn, a late entrant placed there misses theirs, and nobody can dock there. The right dock has two exits. Run: 280 "no legal move" turns in about 60,000.
- **Doing nothing can win.** A total can be negative (`packages/engine/src/economy.ts:92-100`), so a player on $0 beats anyone left holding fine tokens. In 1,000 random games 929 had at least one negative total. Random bots are bad players, but it shows fines outweigh careless fishing.
- **Missed turns for players 3 and 4 did not happen.** `turnSkipped` occurred 0 times in 1,000 random games. That answers the rulebook's open playtest question for now: with ships forced to leave docks, late entrants almost always find one free.
- **The Golden Fish is mostly lost, not won, by careless players.** In the same run it was taken 17 times and lost to a full ship 49 times. Real players will avoid this, but the "full ship removes it for everyone" rule is a strong denial move.

## P3

### Engine

- **Impossible branches return quietly.** `packages/engine/src/apply.ts:183`, `:187`, `:214`, `:219`, `:241` return without doing anything if a legal action meets a state it does not expect. `applyAction` would then report success, add one to `actionCount` and change nothing. They cannot be reached today; they should throw so a future bug is loud.
- **The fine-token invariant depends on the content.** "A player holding fine tokens has $0 cash" (rulebook, Helicopter; architecture §11) holds only while every fish value, fine and price is a multiple of `fineTokenValue`. `compileContent` does not check this (`packages/engine/src/content.ts:240-245`, `:365`). A $150 fish in `cards.json` would allow tokens with cash, which makes "Helicopter with fine tokens" reachable.
- **Redaction is field by field only at the top level.** `packages/engine/src/view.ts:26-29` copies `players`, `turn` and `phase` whole. The comment above it promises more than that; a future private field inside a player or a phase would be sent to everyone.
- **`replay` ignores the log's `schemaVersion`** (`packages/engine/src/replay.ts:10-15`).
- **Dead branch:** `packages/engine/src/eventCards.ts:80-81` handles content with no event cards, which `compileContent` refuses.
- **`SETUP_SHIPS = 2` is in code** (`packages/engine/src/apply.ts:32`), not in `content/rules.json`. The 5–6 player variant in the rulebook will need it, a fourth dock kind and `maxPlayers`.
- **Docs that have drifted:**
  - `docs/architecture.md` §4 says the Helicopter "pays fine tokens from the sale"; item 28 in §12 says that case cannot happen.
  - `docs/rulebook.md` (Docks) lists "pay fine tokens" before "sell"; the engine sells first and then pays (`economy.ts:64-84`). The result is the same; the event order is the other way round.
  - `docs/rulebook-engine-notes.md` still has "Risk (15)" and "Docking: exact landing" as rows; both are corrected by later rows, but a reader who stops early gets the old rule.

### Server

- **No `Origin` check, no limit per IP, no backpressure limit** (known, in CLAUDE.md). Two consequences worth naming:
  - one machine opening 500 sockets can fill the room cap, and then nobody can create a room (`packages/server/src/transport.ts:205-211`);
  - room codes can be walked (19⁴ ≈ 130,000) at 10 messages a second per socket, and `room-not-found` versus `game-started` says which exist (`core.ts:241-245`). A stranger can sit in a lobby, and the host cannot remove them.
- **A socket that never takes a seat lives for ever.** The heartbeat keeps it open (`transport.ts:243-254`); there is no idle limit for unseated connections.
- **Names are not cleaned beyond whitespace** (`core.ts:496-499`): control, zero-width and right-to-left characters pass, and the uniqueness check does not normalise Unicode. React escapes them, so this is confusion, not injection.
- **A disconnected lobby seat holds its name for 2 minutes** (`core.ts:249-251`), so a player who lost their token cannot rejoin under the same name until the seat is freed.
- **`away` is never cleared when a game finishes** (`core.ts:530-535`), so the result screen can show a player as away.
- **Seat order uses `localeCompare`** (`core.ts:515`): correct to `p9`, wrong from `p10`.
- **`main.ts`:** `PORT` is not validated (`packages/server/src/main.ts:15`); shutdown waits on `http.close`, which can hang on keep-alive connections (`main.ts:43-49`).

### Web client (protocol handling only)

- **`?server=` can send the seat token elsewhere.** `apps/web/src/useGame.ts:50-52` uses any URL from the query string, and on load the client sends `rejoin` with the saved token to it (`useGame.ts:151-160`). A link opened in the same tab mid-game hands the seat to whoever runs that server. Restrict it to dev builds or to an allow-list.
- **`leave` while reconnecting is lost.** `useGame.ts:186-189` sets `stop` and sends on a socket that is not open, so nothing is sent; the retry timer already set (`useGame.ts:137-146`) then calls `open`, which clears `stop` (`useGame.ts:100`) and rejoins. `open` never cancels a waiting timer.
- **Errors other than `bad-token` and `room-not-found` during a rejoin leave a dead table.** After `unsupported-version` (for example an old tab after a deploy) the socket stays open with no seat, the old table stays on screen, and every click answers `not-seated` (`useGame.ts:116-125`).
- **The server port is fixed at 8787** (`useGame.ts:54`), and an `https` page needs `wss` on that port. Deployment is Milestone B5; noted so it is not a surprise.

### Missing tests

- **`useGame.ts` has no tests.** Reconnect backoff, the saved session, the `bad-token` reset and `leave` are covered only by `scripts/playtest-three-tabs.mjs`, which is not part of `pnpm check` or CI (`.github/workflows/ci.yml`).
- **The fuzz test does not know what it reached.** `packages/engine/src/fuzz.test.ts` asserts invariants but not coverage. Run: a random bot over 1,000 games never produced `turnSkipped`, `relocationFailed` or a Sinking Boat sent to the cruise dock. Those three are covered only by hand-built unit tests. The fuzz test should count event types and fail if one it expects never appears.
- **The fuzz test hard-codes `100 *` for the token value** (`fuzz.test.ts:130`) instead of reading the content.
- **Engine cases with no test I could find:**
  - the Helicopter expiring between a player's start-of-turn and end-of-turn (last turn of rounds 10 or 15), and its use on the very last turn of round 20;
  - a Sinking Boat victim taking the dock a waiting late entrant needed (the only realistic way to get `turnSkipped`);
  - content whose money values are not multiples of the token value (see Engine, above);
  - `applyAction` leaving its input state untouched, outside the fuzz test's `deepFreeze`.
- **Server cases with no test:**
  - two rooms at once (an intent or a tick in one must not touch the other), and `tick` when one room throws (P2-4);
  - a player whose connection drops repeatedly inside the grace (P1-2);
  - the transport's `close()` during a game;
  - names with unusual Unicode.

## What I looked for and did not find

So the next reviewer need not repeat it:

- **Movement:** `reachableDestinations` matches the rulebook on every point I could construct (no step back, loops over the start tile, docks only as the last step, no dock-to-dock step, early docking only for docks, never ending on the start tile).
- **Money:** fines, tokens, sales, the shop closing, the Helicopter fee from cash only, and scoring all follow the rulebook and the notes log.
- **Cards and events:** copy counts in `content/cards.json` match the rulebook tables (18, 16, 11). Event timing, expiry, Tsunami and the Golden Fish follow the notes log. Only one event is active at a time, so events never combine.
- **Hidden information:** no message, event or view carries the seed, the RNG state or a draw pile's order. The player in an action always comes from the seat.
- **Sequence rules:** one intent per `seq` on the server; the client's handling of old, repeated and skipped updates is correct and tested.
- **Determinism:** no `Math.random`, `Date` or key-order dependence in the engine; `replay` reproduces whole games.
